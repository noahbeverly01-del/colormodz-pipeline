ColorModz Pipeline

A personal production tool for the ColorModz YouTube channel.

ColorModz Pipeline is a self-hosted automation tool used by the owner of the ColorModz YouTube channel to produce and publish videos about home servers and self-hosting. It runs entirely on the channel owner's own hardware and is not offered to, or used by, anyone else.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads finished videos to the ColorModz channel as private, scheduled uploads.
  • Sets the custom thumbnail and uploads the caption track for those videos.
  • Reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics (views, click-through rate, watch time, retention) to learn which videos worked.

It only ever acts on the channel owner's own channel, after the owner signs in with Google and grants access.
